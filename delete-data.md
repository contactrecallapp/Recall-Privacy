# Delete your data — Recall: Food Recall Alerts

This page explains how to delete the data that **Recall: Food Recall Alerts**
stores about your device, what gets deleted, and what does not.

Contact: **ContactRecallApp@gmail.com**

## How to request deletion

The fastest way takes about ten seconds and needs no email:

1. Open **Recall: Food Recall Alerts** on your device.
2. Tap **Settings** in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Scroll to the **Data** section at the bottom.
4. Tap **Delete my data**.
5. Confirm when asked. The deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

If you have already uninstalled the app, or the button does not work, email
**ContactRecallApp@gmail.com** and the data will be deleted for you. Please
send the request from any address — we do not hold your email, so there is
nothing to match it against, and we will simply confirm once it is done.

## What is deleted

Everything stored about your install, on our server and on your device:

| Data | Where | Deleted |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous device identifier | Server and device | Yes, immediately |
| Push notification token | Server and device | Yes, immediately |
| Saved items (the labels and searches you saved) | Server and device | Yes, immediately |
| Allergen selections | Server and device | Yes, immediately |
| Quiet hours and time zone | Server and device | Yes, immediately |
| Theme preference | Device only | Yes, immediately |
| Cached recall data | Device only | Yes, immediately |

There is no retention period and no backup copy held back. The server record is
deleted outright rather than marked inactive, and your saved items are removed
with it automatically.

## What is not deleted

**An empty anonymous sign-in identifier.** The app signs in anonymously so your
saved items can follow you between launches. Deleting your data removes the
record that identifier pointed at, but the identifier itself remains in the
authentication system as an empty shell. It contains a random ID and the date
it was created. It holds no saved items, no allergen selections, no push token,
and nothing that identifies you, and the app never uses it again.

**Public recall data.** Recall stores FDA, USDA, and CDC recall notices so the
app can search them. That is public government information about products, not
information about you, and it is unaffected.

## After you delete

The app keeps working. It creates a fresh anonymous identifier straight away so
searching, scanning, and alerts continue to function — you simply start over
with nothing saved. Deleting your data resets who the app thinks you are, not
whether it works.
